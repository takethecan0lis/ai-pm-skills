# Needs-you block

## Purpose
Decisions get lost when they sit at the bottom of a long status note. This skill moves every decision to the top, caps them at three, and makes each one answerable by typing a few words under it. The reader sees what is blocking progress before anything else.

## When to use
- You are writing a status note, weekly update, hand-off or resume note and at least one item needs a decision from the reader.
- A reader has told you they skim and miss questions.
- Skip it when nothing needs the reader. Then open with the line "Nothing needed from you."

## Steps
1. List every open question for the reader. Delete any you can settle yourself and reverse later, and record those in a "decided by default" line at the bottom of the note instead.
2. Rank the rest by how much each one blocks progress. Keep the top 3. Move the others to an "Also waiting" list, at most 7 lines, each with the date it was first asked.
3. Write each ask as one plain sentence that names the decision in words first and carries any ticket or item number after it, with its meaning in a few words beside the number.
4. Put the recommended answer first, directly after the question. Do not offer a menu of options.
5. Add a blank line reading "Your answer:" under each ask so the reader can type straight into the note.
6. Mark each ask as reserved or not. A reserved ask is one that only the reader may decide (money, accounts, anything sent on their behalf, deletions). It has no default. Any other ask gets a default and a date two days from today: if the reader is silent by that date, the default is taken and the note says so.
7. Ask each question once. If it already sits in the block, it does not reappear in the body or in "Also waiting".
8. Put the block above everything else in the note. News and detail follow it.
9. At the next note, move any non-reserved ask that is two days old to its default and remove it from the block.

## Example
Invented data. Product team "Pebble Notes", note dated 2026-03-09.

Needs you
1. Ship the onboarding checklist to all new users this week, or hold it for the pricing change? Recommended: ship this week. Default if no answer: ship on 2026-03-11.
Your answer:

2. Approve the 4,000-dollar usability study vendor? Reserved: money, no default. Recommended: approve.
Your answer:

Also waiting
- Rename the "Teams" tab to "Groups" (asked 2026-03-08, default on 2026-03-10: keep "Teams").
- Retire the old export format (asked 2026-03-05, default taken today: keep it for one more release).

Decided by default (undo by naming it): moved the beta cohort survey from Tuesday to Wednesday.

## What this does not verify
- It does not check that the asks are the right ones, only that they are visible, capped and answerable.
- It does not make a reader reply; a silent reader triggers the default, which can be wrong.
- Adopted, not measured: no result is claimed for fewer missed decisions or faster replies.
